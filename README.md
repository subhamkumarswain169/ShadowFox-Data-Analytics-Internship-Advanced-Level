## 📈 Dashboard Preview & Key Insights
![Dashboard Overview](./hr_dashboard_overview.png)

1. **Role Vulnerability:** Sales Representatives (39.76%) and Laboratory Technicians (23.94%) experience the highest turnover, while executive roles like Research Directors (2.50%) maintain near-perfect retention.
2. **Burnout Correlation:** Employees working OverTime show an attrition rate of 30.53%, nearly triple the 10.44% observed among non-overtime peers.
3. **Compensation Gradient:** Departures are heavily concentrated in Job Level 1 roles earning below $3,000/month, pointing to early-career compensation dissatisfaction.

---

## 💡 Strategic HR Recommendations
1. **Workload Redistribution:** Implement limits and compensatory off-time policies for departments with high overtime demands.
2. **Early-Career Compensation Adjustments:** Revise entry-level compensation packages and tie them to 6-to-12 month milestone retention incentives.
3. **Structured Career Pathways:** Establish transparent promotion timelines for Lab Technicians and Sales Representatives within their first 18 months.
